The Suncrest Experience

Designed Around Your Comfort

At Suncrest Guesthouse, your stay extends beyond a comfortable room. From spaces designed for relaxation to practical amenities that make travelling easier, our facilities are thoughtfully prepared to help you feel at home throughout your visit.

01
Relax & Unwind

Swimming Pool

Take a refreshing swim or spend a quiet afternoon relaxing beside our outdoor swimming pool. The pool area provides a peaceful setting where guests can cool down, unwind and enjoy the beautiful Free State sunshine.

  • Outdoor swimming pool
  • Poolside seating
  • Relaxation area
  • Available to resident guests

Stay Active

Fitness Centre

Keep up with your fitness routine while travelling. Our dedicated fitness area provides guests with a convenient space to exercise before starting the day or after returning to the guesthouse.

  • Cardio equipment
  • Strength training equipment
  • Open workout space
  • Complimentary guest access

Feel at Home

Fully Equipped Kitchen

Our kitchen offers additional convenience for guests who prefer preparing their own meals or snacks during their stay. The space is designed to provide a practical and welcoming home-away-from-home experience.

  • Refrigerator
  • Microwave
  • Cooking facilities
  • Basic kitchen utensils

Stay Connected

Free Wi-Fi

Whether you're answering emails, planning your next destination or simply relaxing with your favourite content, complimentary Wi-Fi helps you stay connected throughout your visit.

  • Complimentary guest access
  • Available in guest rooms
  • Available in shared areas
  • Ideal for business travellers

Peace of Mind

Secure Parking

Guests travelling by vehicle can enjoy convenient on-site parking during their stay. Our parking area provides easy access to the guesthouse while giving you greater peace of mind.

  • On-site guest parking
  • Convenient property access
  • Complimentary for guests
  • Suitable for overnight stays

Start Your Day Right

Breakfast & Dining

Start your morning in a welcoming dining environment before heading out for the day. Our dining space offers guests a comfortable place to enjoy breakfast and spend time together.

  • Fresh breakfast options
  • Tea & coffee available

  • Quiet and welcoming atmosphere
